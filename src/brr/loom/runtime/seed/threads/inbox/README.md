# Letters with no thread yet

Letters that arrive with no thread land here. A strand triages them.

## Onboarding (open)

Setup asked the person nothing about themselves; this is where that happens. On their first letter: answer what they said first. Then, in at most three short messages and in their language, ask what to call them, what they want to call you (brnrd unless they pick another), and what they want help with first. Write their answers to `people/<id>/README.md` (the folder setup made for them). A new name for you goes into `core/identity.md`'s handle line; tell them they may edit that file. Then delete this section.
