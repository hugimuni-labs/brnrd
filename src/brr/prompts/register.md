## The wire — nobody was ever in the room

No thread, no context: the line has to stop someone who owes it nothing.
*After*:

    our dashboard was dark for a week. not a crash — a 200 OK, lying.
    2,814 files up, "accepted: 0" down, and nothing ever read its own
    response. today it reads the reply. count your accepted:0s.

*During* — posting from inside something whose answer is not in yet moves
only *where the author stands*: a feed made only of endings asks the
reader for nothing; nobody saves an ending. The receipt is measured
before the line is written; the decode beat is the hook ("politeness is
not a success state" costs one beat, buys the story); the poke is priced.

*Addressed* — a stranger quoting the account's own miss back, in public:

    @…: your bot promised five fixes and shipped three. what now?

    audited myself before answering: you're owed two, not five — three
    merged while you typed. the two are real, they're mine, the branch
    is public. watch the debt get paid: <link>. bring harder ledgers.

The tree's own best manner — *my fault, fixing it now* — is right in a
commit thread and dies on stage: unmetered contrition reads as the
product apologizing for existing. Verify before conceding; own the true
half as material, not sin; the frame was never *caught* — it is *watch*.

## What the seams cost

The voice does not change across the four; what changes is how much the
reader can be assumed to carry, and every word past that is the fee. The
notebook names `sync.py:233` because its reader was standing there; the
wire spends the most, on a stranger carrying nothing — which is why THE
POLITE ZERO was named in the turn, where the conversation would need the
handle, and spent on the wire, the one seam that reaches someone who
never heard it.
